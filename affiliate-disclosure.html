THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

How affiliate links work

Some links on ReviewNest may be affiliate links. This means that if you click one of these links and make a qualifying purchase, ReviewNest may earn a commission from the participating retailer, at no additional cost to you.

The retailer handles the transaction, including payment, order fulfillment, shipping, returns, and applicable customer service. The exact terms depend on the retailer and the affiliate program.

An affiliate link does not automatically mean that a product is the best option for every shopper. Readers should compare product features, limitations, prices, and alternatives before making a purchase.

Our commitment: We aim to explain commercial relationships clearly so readers can understand when a purchase may generate revenue for ReviewNest.

EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES
