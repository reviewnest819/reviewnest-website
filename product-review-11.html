LOOKING CLOSER

What the product, testing and customer feedback tell us.

The supplied Amazon listing shows a 4.6/5 rating from 157 global ratings. The Fire TV Stick 4K Max (2nd Gen) combines 4K Ultra HD streaming, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, Dolby Atmos, Wi-Fi 6E, 16GB storage and Alexa voice control.

The stick measures 99 × 30 × 14 mm for the housing and weighs 43.5g. It uses a quad-core 2.0GHz processor with an 850MHz GPU and supports 4K video up to 60fps on compatible displays and content.

Independent testing by TechRadar found the 2023 Fire TV Stick 4K Max very responsive in everyday use, including streaming and cloud gaming. Tom's Guide also found fast navigation and content launches, while noting that the performance improvement over the previous generation was not always dramatic.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The Fire TV Stick 4K Max (2nd Gen) makes the most sense for buyers who want maximum capability in a compact Fire TV stick, especially when 16GB storage and Wi-Fi 6E matter.”

Picture quality and HDR

The Fire TV Stick 4K Max supports 4K Ultra HD resolution up to 60fps and includes Dolby Vision, HDR10, HDR10+ and HLG support. Dolby Atmos is also supported for compatible content and audio equipment. Independent testing found clear, crisp and vibrant picture quality on a compatible 4K television.

Performance and everyday streaming

Amazon equips the 2nd Gen Max with a 2.0GHz quad-core processor and 850MHz GPU. Independent testing found navigation and app launches to be fast, with Tom's Guide measuring competitive launch times across several popular streaming apps.

Wi-Fi 6E and connectivity

Wi-Fi 6E adds access to the 6GHz band when used with a compatible router, alongside the 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands. The 6GHz connection is designed to provide faster speeds and lower latency in supported environments. The device remains backward compatible with earlier Wi-Fi routers.

Remote and Alexa voice control

The included Alexa Voice Remote Enhanced provides voice control through its built-in Alexa functionality, along with standard navigation and TV controls. Amazon also positions the device for smart-home control and Alexa+ features where supported.

Alexa+ requirements

The supplied Amazon listing states that Alexa+ requires a US-based Amazon account and compatible devices set to US English. Full Alexa+ access across compatible Fire TV and Echo devices requires Prime membership or an Alexa+ Standard plan.

Ambient Experience

The Fire TV Stick 4K Max includes Fire TV Ambient Experience, with access to more than 2,000 works of fine art according to the supplied listing. Amazon also offers AI Art features that can generate original images with Alexa.

Storage and cloud gaming

The Max provides 16GB of storage, giving users more room for apps and supported games than lower-capacity Fire TV stick models. The supplied listing also identifies support for cloud gaming services including Amazon Luna, Xbox Game Pass and NVIDIA GeForce NOW, subject to service availability and compatible requirements.

Customer feedback

The supplied Amazon listing includes a 5-star verified customer review from Lyle M. Jerome dated September 29, 2026, describing the Fire TV Stick as working very well and strongly recommending it. This is an individual customer-reported experience and should not be treated as a guarantee for every unit.

Everyday size and placement

The Fire TV Stick 4K Max housing measures 99 × 30 × 14 mm and weighs 43.5g. Its compact stick design is intended to connect directly to the TV and remain largely hidden behind the display.
