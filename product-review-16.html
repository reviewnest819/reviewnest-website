REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The M5 MacBook Air's biggest advantage is the balance between performance, portability and efficiency.”

Performance and everyday use

The M5 chip gives the 13-inch Air enough performance for browsing, productivity, coding, multitasking, creative applications and many demanding everyday tasks. The 16GB unified memory configuration also gives the system a stronger multitasking foundation.

Battery and portability

Apple rates this MacBook Air for up to 18 hours of video streaming and up to 15 hours of wireless web use. At about 2.71 pounds, the 13-inch model is also designed for users who prioritize carrying their laptop frequently.

Display, camera and connectivity

The Liquid Retina display supports 1 billion colors, P3 wide color and True Tone. The 12MP Center Stage camera is designed to keep users in frame during video calls. Connectivity includes Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, two Thunderbolt 4 ports, MagSafe 3 and a 3.5mm headphone jack.

MacBook Air M5 is a strong choice — especially if you want serious performance in a highly portable laptop.

This 13-inch configuration combines the M5 chip, 16GB unified memory, 512GB SSD, Liquid Retina display, Wi-Fi 7 and up to 18 hours of rated video-streaming battery life. It makes the most sense for users who value portability and efficiency without stepping up to a MacBook Pro.
