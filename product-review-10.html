LOOKING CLOSER

What the product, testing and customer feedback tell us.

The supplied Amazon listing shows a 4.4/5 rating from 6,014 ratings. The Roku Streaming Stick 4K supports 4K streaming, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, long-range Wi-Fi and voice control through the included remote.

This Streaming Stick 4K is designed to plug directly into a TV's HDMI port and stay hidden behind the screen. Roku lists support for up to 2160p at 60fps on compatible 4K TVs, with Dolby Vision, HDR10+, HDR10 and HLG support.

Independent testing found the 2021 Streaming Stick 4K noticeably faster to start and responsive when opening and switching between apps. TechRadar also found its long-range Wi-Fi useful for TVs positioned farther from the router.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The Roku Streaming Stick 4K makes the most sense for buyers who want strong 4K HDR picture support and a compact, simple streaming experience at a reasonable price.”

Picture quality and HDR

The Streaming Stick 4K supports 4K resolution up to 60fps and adds Dolby Vision and HDR10+ to the Roku streaming experience. Independent testing found strong picture quality with Dolby Vision content, including good contrast, detail and vivid colors on compatible displays.

Performance and everyday streaming

The 2021 model introduced a newer processor that improved startup speed and app navigation. TechRadar reported roughly a 30% faster boot time than the previous generation and found apps opened quickly during testing.

Long-range Wi-Fi and connectivity

Roku uses an 802.11ac dual-band MIMO Wi-Fi system with a dedicated long-range receiver built into the USB power cable. The design is intended to help when the TV is located farther from the wireless router. The device connects to the TV through HDMI and requires external power.

Remote and voice control

The included Roku Voice Remote provides TV power, volume and mute controls on compatible TVs, along with voice search and control. Roku also supports control through compatible Alexa, Google Assistant and Apple AirPlay/HomeKit setups.

Customer-reported remote pairing

One verified customer review supplied with the listing reported difficulty pairing the remote initially and later resolved it by holding the Home and Back buttons together until the remote's green light blinked. This is a customer-reported experience, not a guarantee that every unit will require the same procedure.

Renewed condition and long-term value

Amazon says Renewed products are inspected, tested, cleaned and refurbished as necessary by qualified suppliers. The supplied listing also states that accessories may be compatible replacements rather than original accessories, and cosmetic condition can vary by Renewed grade.

Everyday size and placement

The Streaming Stick 4K measures about 3.7 × 0.80 × 0.45 inches and weighs about 0.90 ounces. Its slim stick design keeps the player behind the TV and helps avoid blocking neighboring HDMI ports.
