LOOKING CLOSER

What the product and customer feedback tell us.

The supplied Amazon listing shows a 4.8/5 rating from 3,687 global ratings. Customer feedback is particularly positive about the controller's premium feel, smooth operation, ergonomics, comfort and battery life.

The Switch 2 Pro Controller adds several useful features over a basic gamepad, including HD Rumble 2, motion controls, built-in amiibo functionality, a dedicated GameChat C button, a 3.5mm audio jack and programmable GL/GR rear buttons.

Nintendo rates the internal lithium-ion battery for approximately 40 hours of use. Nintendo also lists approximately 3.5 hours for a full charge. Actual battery life can vary depending on usage conditions.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The Switch 2 Pro Controller makes the most sense for players who want premium comfort and Nintendo's full controller feature set.”

Battery and everyday use

Battery life is one of the controller's strongest points. Nintendo rates it at approximately 40 hours per charge, while independent testing has also reported roughly 40 hours of real-world endurance. Charging takes around 3.5 hours according to Nintendo.

Comfort and gameplay

Independent reviews praise the controller's ergonomics, balanced feel and comfortable grip. The smooth sticks, responsive controls and improved design make it particularly well suited to longer docked gaming sessions.

Features that matter

HD Rumble 2, motion controls and built-in amiibo support retain important Nintendo features, while the GL/GR buttons add programmable rear controls. The dedicated C button provides quick access to GameChat on Nintendo Switch 2.

Questions you may have about Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller.

Clear answers based on the supplied product information, customer feedback and verified product details.

RN KNOW
BEFORE YOU BUY.
01 Is this the official Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller? +

Yes. This is the Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller made by Nintendo. The listed model number is BEEAFSSKA and the ASIN is B0F3VTFD6J.

02 Does it work with the original Nintendo Switch? +

No. Nintendo states that the Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller is designed only for use with Nintendo Switch 2.

03 What makes the Switch 2 Pro Controller different? +

Key additions include HD Rumble 2, a C Button for GameChat, programmable GL/GR buttons, motion controls, built-in amiibo functionality and a 3.5mm CTIA audio jack.

04 Can I customize the GL and GR buttons? +

Yes. The GL and GR buttons can be freely mapped to buttons you choose, making them useful for games where extra programmable controls are helpful.

05 Does it have a headphone jack? +

Yes. It has a 4-pole stereo 3.5mm mini plug using the CTIA standard for compatible headphones and headsets.

06 How long does the controller take to fully charge? +

Nintendo states that the Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller takes approximately 3 hours to fully charge. Its internal lithium-ion battery is rechargeable.

07 Is the Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller worth buying? +

It can be a strong choice if you prefer a traditional full-size controller, want programmable rear buttons, HD Rumble 2 and wired headset support. Customer feedback is especially positive about comfort, build feel and controls.

08 What should I check before ordering? +

Check the current price, Nintendo Switch 2 compatibility, included USB-C charging cable, seller/listing details and whether you need features such as programmable GL/GR buttons or the 3.5mm audio jack.
