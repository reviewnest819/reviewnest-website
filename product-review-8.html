LOOKING CLOSER

What the product, testing and customer feedback tell us.

The supplied Amazon listing shows a 4.6/5 rating from 7,016 global ratings. Customer feedback frequently praises sound quality, bass, value, portability and Bluetooth connectivity, while battery life and durability receive more mixed experiences.

The JBL Charge 6 is a portable Bluetooth speaker designed for everyday and outdoor use. It features IP68 water and dust resistance, drop protection, a removable carrying strap, a built-in powerbank and Auracast support for compatible multi-speaker connections.

Independent testing supports its outdoor-focused design. SoundGuys tested the Charge 6 for two weeks and rated its durability highly, while RTINGS also found it well suited to outdoor listening because of its portability and IP68 protection.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The JBL Charge 6 makes the most sense for buyers who want a rugged, portable speaker for outdoor listening, while understanding that advertised battery life can fall significantly at higher listening volumes.”

Battery and everyday use

JBL advertises up to 28 hours of playtime, consisting of 24 hours under normal operation plus up to 4 additional hours through Playtime Boost. SoundGuys measured 13 hours and 15 minutes at 80dB in standardized testing, showing how volume and listening conditions can substantially affect real-world battery life.

Sound quality and volume

Customer feedback frequently praises the Charge 6 for sound quality, bass and volume. Independent testing found a good amount of low-end strength and a well-balanced mid-range and treble, while also noting that the compact speaker does not deliver the deep low-bass impact of a much larger speaker. The companion app also provides a 7-band EQ for sound customization.

Durability and outdoor use

The Charge 6 has an IP68 rating and JBL states that it can survive a 1-meter drop and submersion up to 1.5 meters under lab conditions. Independent testing also found the speaker well suited to outdoor use because of its durable construction, portability and water and dust resistance.

Connectivity and portable features

The speaker supports Bluetooth and USB connectivity and can connect with compatible JBL speakers through Auracast. It can also connect to two devices simultaneously according to independent testing. The built-in powerbank can charge a compatible mobile device, while a removable carrying strap makes the speaker easier to transport.

Everyday portability

At about 988g and measuring approximately 229 × 99 × 94mm, the Charge 6 remains portable while offering more low-end output than smaller speakers. RTINGS found it easy to carry outdoors, although its compact mono design is better suited to personal listening and smaller spaces than large-area sound coverage.

JBL claims up to 28 hours, including Playtime Boost. SoundGuys measured 13 hours and 15 minutes at 80dB from 1 metre, while RTINGS reported around 14 hours in its testing.

06 Can JBL Charge 6 charge a phone? +

Yes. The Charge 6 has a built-in powerbank that can be used to charge a compatible phone or other device. The speaker also supports USB-C charging and USB audio.

07 Is JBL Charge 6 worth buying? +

It is a strong choice if you prioritize portability, outdoor durability, good bass and useful features. The main compromises are real-world battery life at higher volume, limited deep bass and mono playback from a single speaker.
