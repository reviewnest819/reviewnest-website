Quick verdict

The TAGRY X08 offers useful everyday features on paper, including two charging methods, a digital case display and three sizes of ear tips. However, fit and call quality can vary, and the advertised total battery life should be checked carefully rather than treated as a guaranteed result.

Key features

These details are based on the supplied product listing, not on independent laboratory testing by ReviewNest.

Digital battery display

The charging case displays battery information so you can check the remaining charge.

USB-C and wireless charging

The case supports wired USB-C charging as well as compatible wireless charging.

Touch controls

Touch controls and voice-assistant support are listed. Control gestures may take practice to remember.

Three ear-tip sizes

Different tip sizes are included to help users find a more comfortable fit.

IPX5 water resistance

The listed rating is intended for water jets and splashes. It does not mean the earbuds are safe to submerge.

Microphones in both earbuds

The listing describes microphones and CVC noise reduction for calls. This does not guarantee silent calls in noisy places.

Battery life: check the advertised claim

The supplied listing advertises up to 6 hours of playback per earbud charge and up to 60 hours in total. These are manufacturer claims, not results verified by ReviewNest.

The same listing describes a 470mAh charging case with roughly four additional full charges. That description does not, by itself, clearly explain how the total reaches 60 hours. Treat the total as an advertised figure and check the current manufacturer's details before relying on it for long trips.

Actual playback time can change with volume, call use, connection conditions, battery age and other settings.

Pros and cons

Potential advantages

  • USB-C and wireless charging options.
  • Digital battery display on the case.
  • Three ear-tip sizes listed.
  • Touch controls and voice-assistant support.
  • IPX5 water-resistance rating advertised.

Things to consider

  • Earbud fit and comfort may vary between users.
  • Call clarity in background noise is not guaranteed.
  • Touch gestures may take time to learn.
  • Some customer feedback raises connectivity and durability concerns.
  • The advertised 60-hour total needs clarification.

What customer feedback suggests

In the supplied Amazon listing, customers mention sound quality, battery life, comfort and value as positives. Other feedback is mixed on fit, connectivity, call noise and durability.

These are themes from customer reviews, not findings from ReviewNest's own product testing. Individual experiences may differ, and a customer rating does not guarantee the same results for every buyer.

Who might consider the TAGRY X08?

Worth considering if you want

Everyday wireless earbuds with a charging-case display, multiple charging options and replaceable-size ear tips.

Think twice if you need

Consistently clear calls in noisy environments, a guaranteed battery runtime, a secure fit for every ear shape or verified long-term durability.

What to check before buying

Confirm the current price, return policy, warranty coverage in your region and the manufacturer's latest battery-life specifications. Fit and call performance are especially worth checking if you plan to use the earbuds for work calls.
