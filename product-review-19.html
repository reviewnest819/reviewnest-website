What the product and verified specifications tell us.

The supplied listing shows a 5.0/5 rating from 10 global ratings, but no customer review text is available. That means we should not treat the rating as evidence of specific customer experiences.

The bundle combines the Meta Quest 3S with 256GB of storage, Batman: Arkham Shadow and a 4.8-metre Link Cable. The headset supports standalone VR and can connect to a PC for compatible PC VR experiences.

The verified Quest 3S display uses LCD technology with 1832 × 1920 pixels per eye. It supports 72Hz, 90Hz and 120Hz refresh rates and uses Fresnel lenses. Its field of view is approximately 96° horizontally and 90° vertically.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The value of this bundle depends on whether the 256GB storage, Batman game and long Link Cable match the way you plan to use VR.”

Storage and everyday use

The 256GB capacity gives users more internal space for games and apps than the smaller Quest 3S capacity. This can be useful for users who expect to keep a larger VR library installed.

Standalone and PC VR

Quest 3S supports standalone VR experiences without a PC. The included 4.8-metre Link Cable adds a wired connection option for compatible PC VR content, giving the bundle more flexibility than a standalone-only setup.

Display and optics

The Quest 3S uses LCD displays and Fresnel lenses. Verified specifications list 1832 × 1920 pixels per eye, with 72Hz, 90Hz and 120Hz refresh-rate options. Buyers should not rely on the supplied listing's OLED or 4K claims for this headset.

Battery expectations

Quest 3S battery life is around 2.5 hours under typical usage according to published specifications. Actual runtime varies with usage, brightness, wireless activity and the applications being used.
