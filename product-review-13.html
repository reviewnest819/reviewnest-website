LOOKING CLOSER

What the product, specifications and customer feedback tell us.

The supplied Amazon listing shows a 4.8/5 rating from 462 ratings. This Kindle Essentials Bundle includes the Kindle 2024 release in Matcha, a Matcha fabric cover and a 9W power adapter.

The 2024 Kindle is Amazon's lightest and most compact Kindle. It features a 6-inch glare-free 300 ppi display, adjustable front light, dark mode and 16GB of storage. Amazon states that the front light is 25% brighter at maximum setting compared with the previous version.

The device is designed specifically for reading rather than general tablet use. Its distraction-free experience removes typical smartphone and tablet interruptions such as messages, emails and social media while reading.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The 2024 Kindle makes the most sense for readers who want a lightweight, compact e-reader with a sharp glare-free display and a simple distraction-free reading experience.”

Display and reading comfort

The Kindle uses Amazon's 6-inch glare-free display with 300 ppi, optimized font technology and 16-level grayscale. The display includes an adjustable front light and dark mode, allowing text to be inverted for different reading conditions.

Brighter front light

Amazon states that the front light is 25% brighter at maximum setting and is now as bright as the Kindle Paperwhite. This gives readers more flexibility when reading in darker environments while retaining the glare-free e-reader display.

Storage and reading library

The Kindle includes 16GB of storage, which Amazon says can hold thousands of books. The Kindle Store provides access to millions of titles, while Kindle Unlimited is available as an optional subscription with access to more than 4 million titles along with audiobooks, magazines and other content.

Battery life and charging

Amazon states that a single USB-C charge can provide up to 6 weeks of battery life, although actual battery life varies depending on use. The supplied customer feedback is generally positive about battery life, but Amazon's aggregated review summary also indicates mixed opinions from customers.

Lightweight and portable design

Amazon lists the Kindle at approximately 6.2 × 4.3 × 0.32 inches and 5.56 ounces (158g). Its compact size and low weight make it suitable for carrying during travel, commuting or everyday reading.

What's included in the bundle

This Essentials Bundle includes the Kindle 2024 release in Matcha, a matching Matcha fabric cover and a 9W power adapter. The included cover is designed to fit the Kindle and supports a sleep-and-wake experience when opened or closed.

Distraction-free reading

Unlike a smartphone or tablet, the Kindle is designed around reading. Amazon highlights the lack of interruptions from messages, emails and social media, allowing readers to stay focused on their books.

Customer feedback

The supplied customer feedback is strongly positive overall. Customers mention the Kindle's compact size, ease of use, quality and Matcha color. One verified purchaser said the Kindle was easy to carry on road trips and airplanes and reported that the battery lasted a long time. These are individual customer-reported experiences and should not be treated as guarantees for every user.

Sustainability

Amazon states that this Kindle uses 75% recycled plastics, 90% recycled magnesium and 100% recyclable device packaging. These figures are supplied by Amazon as part of the product information.

Kindle Unlimited is optional

A Kindle Unlimited membership is not required to use the device. The Kindle provides access to the Kindle Store without a subscription, while Kindle Unlimited can be added separately for readers who want its larger subscription library.

Kids and family reading

Amazon also states that users can set up a Kids profile with access to a curated library through an Amazon Kids+ subscription. This makes the device more flexible for families who want a dedicated reading experience for younger readers.
