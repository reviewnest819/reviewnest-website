LOOKING CLOSER

What the product, testing and customer feedback tell us.

The supplied Amazon listing shows a 4.4/5 rating from 6,017 ratings. The Roku Streaming Stick 4K combines 4K video, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, long-range Wi-Fi, voice control and access to free, live and premium TV content in a compact TV stick.

The supplied specifications list a TV-stick form factor, HDMI connectivity, 4K video output and 4GB of memory storage capacity. The device is sold here as an Amazon Renewed product rather than as new.

Independent testing by Tom's Guide found the Roku Streaming Stick 4K snappy in everyday use and praised its 4K HDR quality, Dolby Vision support and broad app selection. TechRadar also found faster startup and quicker app switching, while describing the Roku UI as relatively unchanged compared with newer competitors.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The Roku Streaming Stick 4K makes the most sense for buyers who want compact 4K HDR streaming, Dolby Vision and long-range Wi-Fi without moving to a larger player.”

Picture quality and HDR

The Roku Streaming Stick 4K supports 4K Ultra HD up to 60fps along with Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and HLG. Tom's Guide found the picture crisp and vibrant during testing, with strong contrast and detailed 4K imagery on compatible content.

Performance and everyday streaming

Roku added a quad-core processor to this generation and claimed around 30% faster app boot times than the previous Streaming Stick Plus. Independent testing found the device responsive, with Tom's Guide measuring app launch times across Netflix, YouTube, Disney Plus, Peacock, HBO Max, Sling and Spotify.

Long-range Wi-Fi and connectivity

One of the Streaming Stick 4K's main features is its long-range Wi-Fi receiver, positioned in the USB power cable. Roku designed this to help maintain a stronger wireless connection when the TV is farther from the router. TechRadar also found the long-range Wi-Fi useful as part of the device's overall appeal.

Remote and voice control

The included Roku Voice Remote provides voice search and control along with TV power, volume and mute functions. The supplied listing also states compatibility with Siri, Alexa and Google Assistant for supported voice-control functions.

Streaming services and free TV

The supplied listing highlights services including Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max, Apple TV+, Netflix, Sling and Hulu. It also promotes Roku's free and live TV offering, including Roku Originals and more than 200 free Live TV channels on The Roku Channel.

Audio limitations

The Streaming Stick 4K supports Dolby-encoded audio, DTS Digital Surround and digital stereo, but independent testing found that it does not support Dolby Atmos. This is worth considering if immersive home-theater audio is a priority.

Renewed condition

This specific listing is Amazon Renewed. Amazon states that Renewed products are professionally inspected, tested, cleaned and refurbished as necessary by Amazon-qualified suppliers. The purchase is also eligible for Amazon Renewed coverage together with Amazon's Return Policy and A-to-z Guarantee.

Customer feedback

The supplied Amazon reviews include a 5-star verified purchase from Mary Lloyd Peacock, who said the replacement stick works well when functioning. Another 5-star verified purchaser, kimmy806, reported initially getting stuck in remote pairing mode and said the remote paired after holding the Home and Back buttons together for about 10 seconds. These are individual customer-reported experiences and should not be treated as guarantees for every unit.

Compact size and placement

The Roku Streaming Stick 4K is designed to plug directly into the TV's HDMI port and remain hidden behind the display. Tom's Guide lists the stick at approximately 3.7 × 0.8 × 0.5 inches, while the long-range Wi-Fi receiver sits separately within the USB power cable.
