LOOKING CLOSER

What the product, testing and customer feedback tell us.

The supplied Amazon listing shows a 4.2/5 rating from 3,809 global ratings. Customer feedback generally praises the headphones for sound quality, noise cancellation and battery life, while fit, connectivity, durability and value receive more mixed opinions.

The WH-1000XM6 uses Sony's HD Noise Canceling Processor QN3, which Sony says processes the signals from 12 microphones in real time. The headphones also feature Adaptive NC Optimizer, Auto Ambient Sound mode, a six-microphone AI beamforming system and support for High-Resolution Audio and LDAC.

Real-world testing gives a useful picture beyond the advertised specifications. RTINGS measured approximately 31.75 hours of continuous battery life with ANC enabled, while Tom's Guide reported close to 30 hours during its hands-on testing. Tom's Guide also found around 40 hours possible with ANC turned off. Actual battery life will still vary with volume, settings and usage.

REVIEWNEST PRINCIPLE
“The WH-1000XM6 makes the most sense for buyers who want flagship noise cancellation, strong battery life and high-quality wireless audio, while accepting that fit and comfort can vary from person to person.”

Battery and everyday use

Sony advertises up to 30 hours of battery life with noise cancellation enabled. Independent testing broadly supports that claim: RTINGS measured about 31.75 hours with ANC on, while Tom's Guide reported close to 30 hours in its testing. Tom's Guide also found roughly 40 hours with ANC disabled. A quick three-minute charge is advertised to provide up to three hours of playback with a compatible USB-PD adapter.

Noise cancellation and sound quality

Noise cancellation is one of the strongest parts of the XM6. The QN3 processor, 12 microphones and Adaptive NC Optimizer are designed to respond to changing environmental noise. Independent testing found the headphones highly effective at reducing sounds such as transport noise, office chatter and air-conditioning noise. The headphones also deliver a warm sound profile with good audio reproduction accuracy, while LDAC provides higher-quality Bluetooth audio on compatible devices. The main trade-off identified in testing is a more limited soundstage.

Calls and microphone performance

Sony uses a six-microphone AI-based beamforming system to isolate the user's voice and reduce surrounding noise. Independent testing found the microphone system very good, with clear and intelligible voice capture and strong background noise handling in typical environments. Extremely harsh noise can still affect voice clarity.

Comfort and everyday fit

The XM6 uses a redesigned headband and foldable construction with a compact carrying case. Independent testing generally describes the headphones as lightweight and comfortable with relatively low clamping force, making them suitable for long listening sessions. However, the shallow earcups can affect the seal for some users, particularly people who wear glasses. This means comfort and noise isolation can depend on individual head and ear shape.
